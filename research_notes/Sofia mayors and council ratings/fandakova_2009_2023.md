# Yordanka Fandakova (Йорданка Фандъкова) as mayor of Sofia Municipality, Nov 2009 – Nov 2023

Reading guide (applies to every section):
- **[DOC]** = documented fact (court ruling, audit finding, official result, signed contract, official decision). **[SELF]** = the municipality's / mayor's own claim, not independently verified. **[ALLEG]** = allegation, NGO or opposition claim, or press opinion.
- **(snippet)** = the figure comes from a search-result excerpt of the linked page; the page itself could not be opened (capital.bg, results.cik.bg, council.sofia.bg and investor.bg returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool). Treat these as needing a second check before being quoted as exact.
- Items without "(snippet)" were read on the linked page.

## 1. Term dates, party backing, election results (2009, 2011, 2015, 2019)

### Takeaway
Fandakova (GERB) held office 23 Nov 2009 – 13 Nov 2023, winning in the first round in 2009 (partial), 2011 and 2015 and only in a runoff in 2019 (49.98% vs 45.13% for Maya Manolova). Exact vote counts and turnout for 2011 and 2015 could not be confirmed from the Central Election Commission, and sources disagree by small margins.

### Cited Findings
- [DOC] Mayor of Sofia from 23 November 2009 to 13 November 2023; succeeded by Vassil Terziev; party GERB. Before that: MP 14–27 July 2009, Minister of Education 27 July – 19 Nov 2009, deputy mayor of Sofia (culture, education, sport) from 2007 — [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yordanka_Fandakova); [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Йорданка_Фандъкова)
- 2009 partial election, 15 Nov 2009: Fandakova 66.23%, Georgi Kadiev (BSP) 27.71%, turnout 23.17% — [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yordanka_Fandakova). Bulgarian Wikipedia gives rounded "67%" and "24%" turnout, described as record low — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Йорданка_Фандъкова)
- 2009: Kadiev's vote count reported as 71,453 (another Capital piece says "about 70,000") (snippet) — [Capital, 16 Nov 2009](https://www.capital.bg/vestnikut/kapital_prim/2009/11/16/816318_s_malko_no_dostatuchno/)
- 2009 was a partial vote (Boyko Borisov had become prime minister); the new mayor's term ran only until 2011 (snippet) — [Capital, 13 Nov 2009](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2009/11/13/815691_kmetut_ne_e_samostoiatelen_igrach/)
- 2011, 23 Oct 2011: won in the first round with "close to 53%", Kadiev (BSP) 22% (snippet; unclear whether final or partial count) — [Investor.bg](https://www.investor.bg/a/332-ikonomika-i-politika/204770-fandakova-pecheli-sofiya-ot-raz); date confirmed by [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Йорданка_Фандъкова)
- 2015, 25 Oct 2015: won in the first round. Figures conflict: 60.13% — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Йорданка_Фандъкова); 60.17% and 238,500 votes attributed to CIK (snippet) — [CIK results page 2015](https://results.cik.bg/minr2015/tur1/mestni/2246.html); 60.11% (snippet) — [Investor.bg](https://www.investor.bg/a/332-ikonomika-i-politika/292463-fandakova-pecheli-izborite-v-sofiya)
- 2019 first round: Fandakova 36.42%, Maya Manolova (independent, BSP-backed) 27.73%. Council vote: GERB-SDS 32.70%, BSP 18.02%, Democratic Bulgaria 14.51%, Boris Bonev's list 10.63% — [News.bg, 4 Nov 2019](https://news.bg/politics/okonchatelno-49-98-za-fandakova-45-13-za-manolova.html)
- 2019 runoff (100% counted): Fandakova 209,542 votes (49.98%), Manolova 189,176 (45.13%), "I support no one" 4.89% — [News.bg, 4 Nov 2019](https://news.bg/politics/okonchatelno-49-98-za-fandakova-45-13-za-manolova.html)
- 2019: Capital's analysis says she lost about one third of her previous support, more than 76,000 fewer votes than in the preceding election (snippet) — [Capital, 7 Nov 2019](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2019/11/07/3988392_mestnite_izbori_otvud_rezultatite/)

### Inferences
- The trend is a steady erosion of a very large mandate: first-round wins in three elections, then a runoff won without an absolute majority of votes cast (49.98%).
- 238,500 (2015, if confirmed) minus 209,542 (2019 runoff) is about 29,000; the "76,000 fewer" figure therefore most likely compares first rounds. Not verified.

### Gaps
- Official CIK vote counts and turnout for 2011 and 2015, and Sofia turnout in both 2019 rounds, were not retrieved (results.cik.bg blocked the fetch). A "42% runoff turnout" headline on news.bg appears to be national, not Sofia; do not use.
- Fandakova's 2009 absolute vote count not found.
- She did not run in 2023; not sourced here.

## 2. Achievements: metro, waste plant, transport fleet, kindergartens, roads, EU funds, parks

### Takeaway
The metro is the best-documented achievement: 33 stations opened during her term (2012–2021) against 14 that existed when she took office, and the 2015 waste-treatment plant ended landfilling-only disposal. Figures for fleet renewal, kindergartens built and EU funds absorbed come mostly from the mayor's own statements and could not be independently confirmed.

### Cited Findings
**Metro (dates from the Bulgarian Wikipedia article on the Sofia metro)** — [Bulgarian Wikipedia: Софийско метро](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Софийско_метро)
- Before her term: 8 stations at end-2008; 8 May 2009 Vasil Levski Stadium – Mladost 1 (5 stations, 5.7 km) and 8 Sept 2009 Sofia University station opened under her predecessor.
- [DOC] 25 Apr 2012: Mladost 1 – Inter Expo Center/Tsarigradsko shose, 2 stations (line 1).
- [DOC] 31 Aug 2012: line 2 Obelya – James Bourchier, 11 stations, 13 km per bg Wikipedia; the English article gives 11.7 km and 13 stations for M2 as a whole — [English Wikipedia: Sofia Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Metro)
- [DOC] 2 Apr 2015: extension to Sofia Airport, 4 stations (about 4.96 km, a computed value). 8 May 2015: Mladost 1 – Business Park, 3 stations, 2.7 km.
- [DOC] 20 Jul 2016: Vitosha station (line 2).
- [DOC] 26 Aug 2020: line 3, first 8 stations. 24 Apr 2021: line 3 extension to Gorna Banya, 4 stations. English Wikipedia lists M3 at 14.2 km and 15 stations today, which includes a section opened after her term — [English Wikipedia: Sofia Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Metro)
- [SELF] 42.5 km of metro built in her time, "about 80% of the network"; daily riders up from 70,000 (2009) to about 360,000 (2023); 9 km left under construction — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Йорданка_Фандъкова)
- EU grant of EUR 282 million for metro and electric buses reported (snippet; year and programme not confirmed) — [zarata.info](https://zarata.info/%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%84%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%83%D1%87%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B0-282-%D0%BC%D0%BB%D0%BD-%D0%B5%D0%B2%D1%80%D0%BE-%D0%BE%D1%82-%D0%B5%D1%81-%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%BC%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE/)

**Waste treatment plant (Sadinata / Han Bogrov)**
- [DOC] The mechanical-biological treatment plant producing RDF has operated since 12 Sept 2015; capacity 410,000 t of mixed household waste a year (snippet) — [Capital, 14 Sept 2015](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2015/09/14/2609706_zavodut_za_sofiiskiia_bokluk_zaraboti_oficialno/); opening also reported by [Balkan Green Energy News](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/waste-processing-plant-inaugurated-near-sofia)
- Investment close to BGN 300 million; operating cost about BGN 30 million a year (snippet) — [Capital, 3 Jul 2020](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2020/07/03/4086387_mrusnata_taina_na_sofiia/)
- [ALLEG, press analysis] The plant misses its targets: about three times less RDF than planned and twice as much residue sent to landfill (snippet) — [Capital, 3 Jul 2020](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2020/07/03/4086387_mrusnata_taina_na_sofiia/)
- The plant was years late: a 2011 Capital piece is titled "A plant always in the future tense", and in 2012 environmental groups warned Bulgaria might have to return EU money over delays (snippets) — [Capital, 6 Dec 2011](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2011/12/06/1323665_zavod_vinagi_v_budeshte_vreme/); [Capital, 4 Apr 2012](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/2012/04/04/1801582_ekoorganizacii_bulgariia_moje_da_vrushta_evropeiski/)

**Public transport fleet**
- [SELF] Renewed 100% of trolleybuses, 90% of buses and 50% of trams — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Йорданка_Фандъкова)
- [DOC, municipal report] 52 small electric buses with chargers financed by OP Environment, BGN 30.3 million; 25 new trams budgeted for delivery in 2023 (snippet) — [Dnevnik, 16 May 2023](https://www.dnevnik.bg/gradska_sreda/2023/05/16/4484098_fandukova_iska_596_mlnleva_za_kapitalovi_razhodi_prez/amp/)
- [SELF] Fandakova: 40% of Sofians' local taxes go to public transport (snippet) — [Dir.bg](https://dnes.dir.bg/obshtestvo/fandakova-40-ot-danatsite-na-sofiyantsi-otivat-za-gradskiya-transport)

**Kindergartens**
- [SELF] Fandakova says she leaves 130 kindergartens built and 21 under construction; in 2022 she put the shortage for ages 3–6 at about 1,500 places and promised to close it by the end of the term (snippet; what "130" counts — new buildings, extensions or both — is unclear) — [sofia.bg](https://www.sofia.bg/w/%D1%84%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B4%D1%8A%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0-%D0%B2-%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B9-%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%BE%D0%BD-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%84%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D1%83%D1%81%D0%BF%D1%8F%D1%85%D0%BC%D0%B5-%D0%B4%D0%B0-)
- 2023 capital programme: BGN 596 million requested in total; BGN 67 million for kindergartens, nurseries and schools, of which BGN 35.8 million for 32 new kindergarten buildings and BGN 10.2 million for 8 nurseries (snippet) — [Dnevnik, 16 May 2023](https://www.dnevnik.bg/gradska_sreda/2023/05/16/4484098_fandukova_iska_596_mlnleva_za_kapitalovi_razhodi_prez/amp/)
- Remaining shortage: in 2020 more than 10,000 children were again left out at the first admission round for kindergartens and nurseries (snippet) — [Fakti.bg](https://fakti.bg/bulgaria/1056110-i-otnovo-hiladi-deca-ostavat-izvan-klasiraneto-za-asli-i-gradini). After her term the numbers stayed similar: more than 10,000 in 2024, 9,700 in 2025 (snippets) — [News.bg](https://news.bg/education/nad-9-700-detsa-ne-vlizat-v-gradini-v-sofiya-na-parvo-klasirane-ostavat-3-200-mesta.html)
- In 2012 Fandakova herself named the kindergarten shortage among the city's four main problems (snippet) — [Capital, 4 May 2012](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/redakcionni_komentari/2012/05/04/1820799_jelanie_i_volia/)
- A council question records non-fulfilment of the council-adopted Kindergarten Construction Programme 2021–2023 in Ovcha Kupel / Gorna Banya; the mayor's team cited lack of plots and money as the main obstacles (snippet) — [Sofia Municipal Council document, 6 Jul 2023](https://council.sofia.bg/documents/d/guest/2023-07-06-soa23-vk66-6488)

**Roads**
- Northern Speed Tangent: tender estimate about BGN 300 million (2012), contract won by Italian consortium Salini–Impregilo in 2013 (snippets). This was a state (Road Infrastructure Agency) project, not a municipal one — [Capital, 1 Nov 2012](https://www.capital.bg/biznes/2012/11/01/1938326_magistralna_vruzka_s_evropa_do_2015_g/); [Capital, 12 Mar 2013](https://www.capital.bg/biznes/kompanii/2013/03/12/2021181_italiansko_obedinenie_pecheli_severniia_skorosten_put/)
- [SELF] Six grade-separated junctions built; video surveillance system with more than 9,500 cameras; four museums opened; "Kultura" grant programme launched — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Йорданка_Фандъкова)

### Inferences
- Stations opened during her term (25 Apr 2012 – 24 Apr 2021): 2 + 11 + 4 + 3 + 1 + 8 + 4 = 33. With 14 stations existing in Nov 2009, the network had 47 stations at the end of 2023. This is a count derived from the Wikipedia opening list, not a published total.
- The "42.5 km" and ridership figures are the mayor's own; the station count is consistent with roughly 70% of today's stations opening in her term. Metro construction was run by the municipal company Metropolitan and funded mainly by EU operational programmes and EIB loans, so credit is shared with the state and the EU.
- The Northern Speed Tangent should not be credited to (or blamed on) the mayor without qualification, since it was state-built.
- Kindergarten evidence points both ways: substantial building activity, yet about 10,000 children unplaced at first ranking both during and after her term (the bulk of the shortage is in nursery age groups, per later municipal data).

### Gaps
- Metro cost per stage and EU funding share: not found in the pages read. The Metropolitan site (metropolitan.bg) was not consulted.
- Opening date, length and cost of the Northern Speed Tangent, and of municipal road projects (e.g., boulevard reconstructions, ring-road sections): not sourced.
- Exact fleet numbers (trams, trolleybuses, electric buses bought, by year and supplier): not found.
- EU funding share of the waste plant, total EU funds absorbed by the municipality 2009–2023, and anything on parks: no reliable source found.
- Independent count of kindergartens built and places added 2009–2023: not found; the Spasi Sofia shortage report (https://spasisofia.org/proekt/doklad-otnosno-nedostiga-na-mesta-v-yaslite-i-detskite-gradini-v-stolichnata-obsthina/) exists but was not read.

## 3. Documented failures and scandals

### Takeaway
Three failures are documented by courts, prosecutors or official sanctions: the 2018–2019 Graf Ignatiev / "Zone 2" repair (fines, an EU-funds financial correction, a deputy mayor indicted), the lost air-quality class action (Sofia City Court, 2021), and the RDF incinerator, whose environmental assessment was annulled in court and whose EU grant was given up. Several other items in the brief (Dondukov, Zone B-18, chief architect, in-house contracts, parking zones, procurement concentration) were not sourced in this pass.

### Cited Findings
**Graf Ignatiev street / central "Zone 2" repair** — main source [Svobodna Evropa, 13 Jun 2019](https://bulgaria.rferl.org/a/29997674.html)
- [DOC] Works started 8 May 2018; original deadline March 2019 (300 days); declared finished 29 May 2019. Contractor GP Group (Джи Пи Груп); designer A.D.A.; supervision by consortium "Project Supervision Sofia – Center".
- [DOC] Project value BGN 20.1 million excl. VAT; more than BGN 14.5 million from EU OP "Regions in Growth"; about BGN 6 million from the municipal budget for tram track and networks. About 40,000 sq m repaired, average BGN 340 per sq m.
- [DOC] Sanctions imposed by the municipality totalled about BGN 567,000, 2.8% of project value: GP Group about BGN 279,000 (defective paving over the tram track), about BGN 110,000 (uneven joints on 750 sq m), BGN 57,000 (five damaged trees), BGN 3,000 (site not cleaned); designer BGN 35,000; supervisor BGN 86,200.
- [DOC] No delay penalty was charged, because the municipality accepted 86 of the 300 days as rain days. Contract terms had been publicised by the mayor as BGN 240,000 per day of delay and BGN 1.2 million for poor quality (snippet) — [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/105692-Rekonstruktsiyata-na-ul-Graf-Ignatiev-zapochva-ot-ponedelnik)
- [DOC] The Ministry of Regional Development imposed a 5% financial correction on the EU grant, citing restrictive qualification requirements in the tender; the municipality appealed to the Administrative Court Sofia-City.
- About 15,000 of the 40,000 sq m had quality problems identified by the supervisors at handover; some defects were left unrepaired.
- [DOC] Deputy mayor for transport and construction Evgeni Krusev resigned on 4 Dec 2018 after being summoned by prosecutors over public-procurement violations and substitution of the vibration-insulation material under the tram track; Doncho Barbalov replaced him (snippets) — [Capital, 4 Dec 2018](https://www.capital.bg/kakvo_stava/2018/12/04/3356155_zamestnik-kmetut_na_sofiia_evgeni_krusev_podade/); [Capital, 6 Dec 2018](https://www.capital.bg/kakvo_stava/2018/12/06/3357284_doncho_barbalov_e_noviiat_zam-kmet_po_stroitelstvoto_i/)
- [DOC] Krusev was later indicted for malfeasance in office over the repair (snippet; an indictment, not a conviction) — [BNR](https://bnr.bg/en/post/101056463/former-deputy-mayor-of-sofia-evgeni-krusev-indicted-for-malfeasance)
- [DOC] In 2023 the European Public Prosecutor's Office was checking the repair's use of EU funds (snippet) — [Svobodna Evropa](https://www.svobodnaevropa.bg/a/32291762.html)
- [ALLEG] Press reported the street needed repair again after reopening (snippet) — [Sega](https://www.segabg.com/category-first-page/grafa-veche-plache-za-remont)
- [ALLEG, press framing] Capital called the 2018 repair scandals "the cracked showcase of GERB" (snippet) — [Capital, 19 Oct 2018](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2018/10/19/3329726_propukanata_vitrina_na_gerb/)

**RDF incinerator at Toplofikatsiya Sofia**
- [DOC] Planned combined heat and power plant burning about 180,000 t of RDF a year. EIB signed a EUR 67 million loan in December 2018; the European Commission approved EUR 77 million from the ERDF in March 2020; total cost about EUR 180 million — [CEE Bankwatch, 26 Sep 2023](https://bankwatch.org/press_release/bulgarian-court-rules-out-sofia-waste-incinerator-plant-due-to-unassessed-health-risks-and-lack-of-public-consultation)
- Earlier figures differ: EUR 158 million total, EUR 91 million EU grant, EUR 67 million loan (snippet) — [Capital, 15 Jun 2018](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2018/06/15/3200177_svetlina_poveche_svetlina/)
- [DOC] The Municipal Council decided to return the EU funding after it became clear the 2023 completion deadline would be missed owing to legal, administrative and technical delays — [CEE Bankwatch, 26 Sep 2023](https://bankwatch.org/press_release/bulgarian-court-rules-out-sofia-waste-incinerator-plant-due-to-unassessed-health-risks-and-lack-of-public-consultation). One source gives the returned amount as EUR 35 million (snippet; conflicts with the EUR 77 million approved) — [Za Zemiata](https://www.zazemiata.org/victory-for-citizens-court-puts-an-end-to-contentious-incineration-project-in-sofia/)
- [DOC] Sofia Administrative Court annulled the environmental impact assessment (unassessed health risks, inadequate public consultation) after about eight years of litigation — [CEE Bankwatch, 26 Sep 2023](https://bankwatch.org/press_release/bulgarian-court-rules-out-sofia-waste-incinerator-plant-due-to-unassessed-health-risks-and-lack-of-public-consultation). The Supreme Administrative Court is reported to have rejected the project definitively on 2 May (2024, i.e. after her term) (snippet) — [OBC Transeuropa](https://www.balcanicaucaso.org/en/?p=236398)
- [DOC] Toplofikatsiya Sofia terminated a BGN 292.2 million construction tender (snippet; year not confirmed) — [SeeNews](https://seenews.com/news/sofia-heating-utility-terminates-waste-incinerator-tender-814803). In 2021 a consortium with Chinese participation had been ranked first (snippet) — [Capital, 28 Jul 2021](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/gradove/2021/07/28/4237005_miks-konstrukshun_i_kitaici_shte_stroiat_instalaciiata/)

**Air pollution**
- [DOC] CJEU, case C-488/15, 5 April 2017: Bulgaria breached the air-quality directive by systematically exceeding daily and annual PM10 limits from 2007 to 2014 in zones including Sofia. The judgment is against the state, not the municipality — [SeeNews](https://seenews.com/news/eu-court-of-justice-rules-against-bulgaria-on-air-quality-564210); (snippet) [Capital, 5 Apr 2017](https://www.capital.bg/kakvo_stava/2017/04/05/2948185_bulgariia_beshe_osudena_v_ljuksemburg_zaradi_mrusniia/)
- [DOC] July 2019: Sofia City Court ordered interim measures — washing main streets twice a month May–September and once a month otherwise, and daily publication of PM readings; the municipality said it would not appeal (snippet) — [Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/198382/The+Court%3A+Sofia+Municipality+Must+Wash+the+Neighbourhood+Streets+Twice+a+Month); [BNT](https://www.bnt.bg/en/a/court-ordered-sofia-municipality-to-take-urgent-measures-to-tackle-dirty-air)
- [DOC] November 2021: Sofia City Court ruled in the class action brought by citizens with the Clean Air Group (Za Zemiata, Spasi Sofia, Center for Independent Living, Type 2 Diabetes Association) that the municipality is responsible for regular harmful pollution levels in 2015–2017, had not updated its air-quality programme and had not met its duty to inform the public. It ordered incentives for cleaner heating, low-emission zones and traffic restrictions on high-pollution days, within one year — [Balkan Green Energy News, 15 Nov 2021](https://balkangreenenergynews.com/city-of-sofia-loses-court-case-over-high-levels-of-air-pollution/)
- The mayor approved BGN 108,000 for a law firm to argue the municipality was not responsible (snippet) — [EPHA guest article](https://epha.org/clean-air-in-bulgaria-mayor-of-sofia-paid-lawyers)
- [ALLEG] Za Zemiata said the authorities had not complied by the 25 Nov 2022 deadline (snippet) — [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/373118-environmental-organization-says-sofia-authorities-fail-to-comply-with-court-ruli)
- Council adopted an air-quality programme for 2021–2026 on 17 Dec 2020 (Decision 64, Protocol 25) (snippet) — [Sofia Municipal Council, Decision 590/2024](https://council.sofia.bg/documents/d/guest/r-590-2024)

**Toplofikatsiya Sofia (municipally owned district heating company)**
- 2012: Capital reports the company "sank into debt"; 2014: among the four largest debtors to Bulgargaz; 2018: agreement for part of its electricity revenue to go directly to Bulgargaz while new arrears accumulated (snippets) — [Capital, 26 Jun 2012](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2012/06/26/1854383_toplofikaciia_-_sofiia_potuna_v_dulgove/); [Capital, 25 Mar 2014](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2014/03/25/2268610_chetiri_toplofikacii_sa_nai-golemite_dlujnici_na/); [Capital, 19 Jul 2018](https://www.capital.bg/kakvo_stava/2018/07/19/3284370_bulgargaz_shte_pribira_direktno_chast_ot_prihodite_na/)
- [ALLEG, NGO reports] Greenpeace Bulgaria: total debt about BGN 1.6 billion; debts to Bulgargaz and BEH above BGN 1.2 billion (snippets; reference years not stated) — [Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org/bulgaria/press/9604/5-zadachi-za-toplofikaciq/); [Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org/bulgaria/press/11477/doklas-toplofikatsia-predstavyane/)

**Other criticism**
- [ALLEG] Boris Bonev (Spasi Sofia): her team lost "270 million" under the Recovery and Resilience Plan for sewerage; general criticism of weak construction supervision, favoured contractors, cracked pavements and inflated prices — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Йорданка_Фандъкова)
- April 2016: Fandakova demanded the resignation of the management of the Urban Mobility Centre (snippet) — [Capital, 22 Apr 2016](https://www.capital.bg/kakvo_stava/2016/04/22/2748067_iordanka_fandukova_e_poiskala_ostavkata_na/)
- [ALLEG] Later reporting mentions a pre-trial proceeding over construction quality of the Northern Speed Tangent (a state project) (snippet, low-reliability source) — [LinkedIn post](https://cy.linkedin.com/in/krasen-stanev-60320655)
- [ALLEG, press analysis] Capital long-reads "12 years of inaction" (26 Mar 2021) and "Sofia – the lost city" (19 Aug 2022) argue the city stagnated; both are paywalled and were not read — [Capital 2021](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/gradove/2021/03/26/4189923_12_godini_bezdeistvie/); [Capital 2022](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/gradove/2022/08/19/4354558_sofiia_-_zagubeniiat_grad/)

### Inferences
- On Graf Ignatiev, the documented picture is: defects officially acknowledged on more than a third of the area, penalties far below the publicised contract maximums, an EU-funds correction for a restrictive tender, and criminal proceedings against the responsible deputy mayor that had not ended in a verdict in the sources seen.
- The incinerator represents a decade of planning with no asset delivered and the EU grant forgone; the amount actually returned or lost is uncertain (EUR 35 million vs EUR 77 million vs EUR 91 million in different sources, likely reflecting disbursed vs approved vs planned amounts).
- The 2017 CJEU judgment is against Bulgaria; the 2021 Sofia City Court ruling is the one directed at the municipality itself.

### Gaps
- Not sourced at all in this pass, so nothing should be asserted: Dondukov boulevard repair (only a general mention of defects and delays in a snippet), other deputy-mayor resignations in 2017–2018, chief architect controversies (Petar Dikov, Zdravko Zdravkov — only article titles seen), overbuilding and urban-plan disputes, in-house contracts, paid parking zones, Zone B-18, the Prince's Garden dispute, procurement concentration figures for GP Group or Trace.
- Whether the November 2021 air ruling was appealed and its final status; exact ruling date.

## 4. National Audit Office (Сметна палата) findings on Sofia Municipality

### Takeaway
One performance audit was read in full: the 2017 report on PM pollution measures (2010–2015) found partial implementation and continued exceedances and addressed five recommendations to the mayor. Other audits were located but not read.

### Cited Findings
- [DOC] Performance audit "Effectiveness of the measures to reduce PM10 and PM2.5 pollution in Sofia", period 1 Jan 2010 – 31 Dec 2015, published 24 Apr 2017. Findings: measures reduced average annual PM10 but the annual limit was not met at all monitoring points; of 52 PM10 measures in the 2011–2014 programme, 38 (73%) were implemented; the 2015–2020 programme was drafted only in December 2016 and still awaited council adoption; respiratory illness among adults was rising per the regional health inspectorate. Two recommendations to the council, five to the mayor (strengthen control over implementation and assign personal responsibility). The audit notes that transport and solid-fuel heating emissions also require national action — [National Audit Office press release](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/en/news/press-releases/neobhodimi-sa-oshte-merki-za-po-chist-vyzduh-v-sofiq/)
- [DOC] 2015 annual PM10 averages cited in the audit: Mladost 55.04 (limit 40), Nadezhda 34.60, Hipodruma 38.09, Kopitoto 15.96 — [National Audit Office press release](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/en/news/press-releases/neobhodimi-sa-oshte-merki-za-po-chist-vyzduh-v-sofiq/)
- [DOC] Financial audit of the 2019 consolidated annual financial statement: unmodified opinion ("true and fair view" as at 31 Dec 2019) (snippet) — [Audit report 0400112819](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/media/documents/gfo-so-2019.pdf)
- [DOC] Audit report 0200002013 on management and disposal of municipal property: found no regulation of the powers of the mayor, district mayors and village mayors in insuring municipal property (snippet; audited period and full findings not read) — [Audit report](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/media/documents/doklad-imoti-stolichna-2014.doc)

### Inferences
- The 2017 audit corroborates, from an official source, the court's later finding that the air-quality programme was not kept up to date.
