## 1. Urban planning and overbuilding (chief architects, Golden Century, Mladost, permits)

### Takeaway
The best-documented case is the "Golden Century" (Златен век) tower: the permit dates from 2007 and was re-certified in 2012 by chief architect Petar Dikov, the state building inspectorate (ДНСК) twice halted the site, and the courts sided with the developer each time. For Mladost, the 2017 referendum/moratorium request was rejected and the district mayor elected on the anti-construction wave was later convicted of bribery. No building-permit or dwellings-completed series was obtained.

### Cited Findings
**Golden Century (Златен век), Lozenets**
- [DOC] The permit for the 34-storey building was issued about ten years before 2017 by then chief architect Petar Dikov, and took effect months before the detailed plan (ПУП) for the area was adopted. In 2012 Dikov re-certified it for five more years, on the basis of a site-opening protocol from January 2010; by law the rough construction should have been finished by end-2017 (read) — [Svobodna Evropa, June 2021](https://www.svobodnaevropa.bg/a/31318725.html)
- [DOC] In 2017 Parliament, on a GERB proposal, extended the validity of such permits from 5 to 10 years (the "Artex amendment"). Construction was stopped in March/April 2019 after the "Apartment-gate" scandal (senior GERB politicians had bought flats from developer Artex below market prices) (read) — [Svobodna Evropa, June 2021](https://www.svobodnaevropa.bg/a/31318725.html)
- [DOC] Administrative Court Sofia-City ruled that the 2017 amendment applies to the tower, so the permit was valid at least until 2022 (until 2028 under one reading of Supreme Administrative Court practice). The article notes that chief architect Zdravko Zdravkov had relied on a deputy minister's interpretation to say the building had valid papers, and changed position after the scandal broke (read) — [Svobodna Evropa, 2019](https://www.svobodnaevropa.bg/a/30087003.html)
- [DOC] In June 2021 the new ДНСК head Vladi Kalinov ordered a second halt, stating the permit had expired in January 2020 and that everything built after that date must be demolished; he also said "Sofia Municipality has also supported Artex" [ALLEG, official's statement]. Lozenets district mayor Konstantin Pavlov referred the continued construction to the chief prosecutor (read) — [Svobodna Evropa, June 2021](https://www.svobodnaevropa.bg/a/31318725.html)
- [DOC] The Supreme Administrative Court (ВАС) confirmed with a final order the Administrative Court's ruling of 25 June 2021 suspending the ДНСК stop order. This is an interim measure in Artex's lawsuit seeking to establish that the permit is valid until December 2024 or September 2030; it is not a final ruling on validity. The first halt (April 2019 – May 2021) had been declared unlawful by ВАС; Artex claimed losses above BGN 18 million (read) — [Svobodna Evropa, July 2021](https://www.svobodnaevropa.bg/a/31361634.html)
- Artex earlier put its losses from the stoppage at BGN 1.8 million (about BGN 120,000 a week) (read) — [Svobodna Evropa, 2019](https://www.svobodnaevropa.bg/a/30087003.html)

**Mladost protests and the moratorium request (2017)**
- [DOC] The "For a Green Mladost" committee asked for a local referendum on a moratorium on building permits in Mladost until the plans were changed. The civil-registry check validated 8,800 signatures against 9,400 needed for a binding referendum (snippet) — [Capital, 31 Mar 2017](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2017/03/31/2944752_da_vurnesh_mladost/)
- [DOC] In March 2017 the council's committees on spatial planning and on environment gave a negative opinion, arguing that a local referendum cannot decide matters regulated by statute; construction was not stopped (snippet) — [manager.bg](https://manager.bg/blgariya/obshchinski-komisii-othvrliha-iskaneto-na-kv-mladost-za-referendum); [Capital, 29 Mar 2017](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2017/03/29/2944457_stolichnata_obshtina_niama_da_dopusne_referendum/)
- [SELF] Chief architect Zdravkov said a moratorium would expose the municipality to owners' claims of about BGN 240 million (another source says BGN 260 million) (snippet) — [gradat.bg](https://gradat.bg/news/2017/03/29/2944154_v_jk_mladost_shte_se_stroi_referendumut_na_ivancheva)
- [DOC] While the moratorium was being refused, the council kept approving individual plot plans in Mladost, e.g. Decision 163 of 6 April 2017 approving a regulation plan for a plot in Mladost 2 (read) — [Sofia Municipal Council, Decision 163/2017](https://council.sofia.bg/documents/20182/376823/R163-06.04.2017.pdf/9f1fee27-fa5f-4990-9826-aeb1a39530df)
- [DOC] Desislava Ivancheva, chair of the committee and then independent mayor of Mladost district (elected against the GERB candidate), was arrested on 17 April 2018 with her deputy Bilyana Petrova. Sentences: 20 years at first instance (April 2019), 12 years on appeal (July 2020; some outlets say 8), and a final 6 years from the Supreme Court of Cassation in November 2022 for requesting and receiving a bribe from developer Aleksandar Vaklin; Petrova 5 years, former district mayor Petko Dyulgerov 4 years (read) — [Svobodna Evropa, Nov 2022](https://bulgaria.rferl.org/a/32123769.html). She was a district mayor in opposition to the city administration, not a member of Fandakova's team.

**Chief architects**
- [DOC] Zdravko Zdravkov was chief architect from May 2016 to July 2024, appointed after a competition; from Oct 2018 to 2020 he also chaired the board of Metropolitan EAD (read; the Wikipedia article is flagged as promotional in tone) — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Здравко_Здравков_(архитект))
- [DOC] A prosecution investigation against Zdravkov, opened after he refused to continue the permit process for a skyscraper in the Strelbishte area, was cancelled in May 2018 (snippet) — [Capital, 23 May 2018](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2018/05/23/3184073_otmeniha_razsledvaneto_sreshtu_glavniia_arhitekt_na/)
- [SELF] In 2017 the municipality initiated changes to the Sofia planning act (ЗУЗСО) to limit building heights and "speculative" exceeding of parameters (read) — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Здравко_Здравков_(архитект))
- [DOC] ВАС ruled that a construction ban on the "Nove Homes" complex was unlawful (snippet, headline only; October 2021) — [Capital, 12 Oct 2021](https://www.capital.bg/biznes/imoti/2021/10/12/4264326_vas_zabranata_za_stroitelstvo_na_kompleksa_nove_homes/)

**Planning and kindergartens**
- [ALLEG, councillor's report] New districts such as Manastirski Livadi and Krastova Vada were densely built without kindergartens being planned first (read) — [Spasi Sofia report, 14 Sep 2020](https://spasisofia.org/proekt/doklad-otnosno-nedostiga-na-mesta-v-yaslite-i-detskite-gradini-v-stolichnata-obsthina/). Fandakova herself said in October 2020 that the construction boom of the last decade produced large districts "without any infrastructure, including educational" in Vitosha, Triaditsa and Lozenets (read) — [bTV, 21 Oct 2020](https://btvnovinite.bg/bulgaria/67-novi-detski-gradini-v-sofija-do-2023-g.html)

### Inferences
- In the Golden Century case the documented decisions run against the inspectorate: both stop orders were overturned or suspended. The permit itself, and its 2012 re-certification, are acts of the municipal chief architect; the 2017 extension is an act of Parliament.
- The Ivancheva conviction concerns a district-level opponent of the city administration; it is evidence of corruption risk around building permits in Sofia, not of wrongdoing by the mayor's team.

### Gaps
- Building permits issued and dwellings completed per year in Sofia (NSI regional statistics, Sofiaplan): not retrieved. No number should be quoted.
- Petar Dikov's departure (2016) and specific controversies: the search could not run. Zone B-18, construction in Borisova gradina and the "Prince's Garden" case: nothing found in this round; nothing should be asserted.
- Final outcome of Artex's main lawsuit on the permit's validity, and whether the tower was completed.
- Exact dates of the 2019 Administrative Court ruling and the 2021 ВАС order were not captured from the pages.

## 2. Public procurement: concentration, in-house awards, audit findings

### Takeaway
The Audit Office's procurement audit for 2010–Sept 2011 found that only 31% of the sampled open procedures were lawful and that restrictive requirements were set in several tenders; a 2021 Ministry of Finance dataset showed BGN 605 million paid on 228 contracts in 2020, with a municipal in-house builder among the top three recipients. No Competition Commission or АДФИ findings were located.

### Cited Findings
- [DOC] Audit Office, "Audit of the award and performance of public procurement in Sofia Municipality", period 1 Jan 2010 – 30 Sep 2011 (report file dated 25 Oct 2012). In the period the municipality ran 126 procedures under the Public Procurement Act (ЗОП), 73 under the small-procurement ordinance (НВМОП) and 350 small awards. Of 26 procedures checked (20 ЗОП, 6 НВМОП): only 31% of the ЗОП procedures were conducted lawfully; two ЗОП procedures and three of six НВМОП procedures (50%) contained requirements that gave advantage or unjustifiably restricted participation; two negotiated procedures without notice were opened unlawfully; security services were paid for without any procurement procedure; one bidder was admitted although its offer did not meet the conditions; two contracts were signed without performance guarantees; some small awards were made without collecting three offers. Cause named by the auditors: ineffective preliminary control (read) — [Audit Office report](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/bg/documents/572/Oditen.doklad_SO_251012.doc); follow-up on recommendations, May 2013 (not read) — [follow-up report](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/bg/documents/3785/Doklad_PK_SO-280513.doc)
- [DOC] Ministry of Finance data presented in July 2021: in 2020 Sofia Municipality made payments on 228 contracts worth BGN 605 million. Top recipients: municipal Metropolitan EAD (one contract, BGN 50 million); municipal Sofekostroy EAD (18 contracts, BGN 46 million); Trace Group Hold (4 contracts, BGN 38 million). The municipality did not give the ministry the number of bidders per tender, saying it needed more time to collect data from 24 districts and over 60 spending units (read) — [Mediapool, 9 Jul 2021](https://www.mediapool.bg/stolichnata-obshtina-malchi-pravi-li-targove-samo-s-edin-kandidat-news323964.html)
- [DOC] On 14 January 2021 the council converted the municipal cleaning company Chistota Iskar into Sofekostroy, adding construction to its activities. [ALLEG] Boris Bonev (Spasi Sofia): the company was given, without tender, the repair of the primates section of the zoo and a contract for 56 playgrounds (read) — [Mediapool, 9 Jul 2021](https://www.mediapool.bg/stolichnata-obshtina-malchi-pravi-li-targove-samo-s-edin-kandidat-news323964.html)
- [DOC] Graf Ignatiev tender: prosecutors' indictment alleged procurement-law breaches causing damage of about BGN 920,000; GP Group's offer was BGN 517,661.69 higher than that of PST, which had been excluded (snippet; the page now returns 404) — [OFFNews](https://m.offnews.bg/news/Obshtestvo_4/Zapochva-deloto-sreshtu-Evgeni-Krusev-za-remonta-na-Grafa_707038.html). The official was acquitted at all three instances (see section 3).
- [DOC] A Trace Group Hold / Trace BG consortium won a 2018 traffic-organisation contract worth BGN 13,812,500 excl. VAT and later the larger part of the follow-on order (snippet) — [Actualno](https://www.actualno.com/sofia/jasno-e-koj-shte-izpylnjava-po-goljamata-chast-ot-porychkata-za-signalizacija-markirovki-i-parapeti-v-sofija-news_1479551.html)
- [DOC] Audit Office audit of the Urban Mobility Centre (2014 – mid-2016) covers procurement at that municipal company; see section 10.

### Inferences
- Two of the three largest 2020 recipients were the municipality's own companies, so "concentration" in that year is partly in-house assignment rather than private contractors.
- The only official quantified finding on procurement legality is from 2010–2011; nothing comparable was found for 2012–2023.

### Gaps
- Shares of GP Group, Trace, PST or Georgi Vasilev-linked firms in municipal contracts over the whole period: no dataset or analysis found (Anti-Corruption Fund and Capital analyses were not reachable).
- Commission for Protection of Competition and АДФИ findings on the municipality: none found; not searched exhaustively.

### Cited Findings
**Krusev case**
- [DOC] The Supreme Court of Cassation upheld the acquittals by the Specialised Criminal Court and its appellate court; Krusev had been charged with unlawfully signing additional contracts in 2018 for the tram-track repair on Graf Ignatiev. The cassation prosecutor did not support the protest in court. Reported 27 May 2022 (read) — [BNR, 27 May 2022](https://bnr.bg/horizont/post/101653483/evgeni-krusev)

Prosecutors had sought five years in prison (read; the outlet's tone is openly critical of the prosecution) — [Club Z, 27 May 2022](https://www.clubz.bg/128356-i_bivshiyat_zam_na_fandakova_evgeni_krusev_okonchatelno_nevinen); (snippet) [BNR](https://old-news.bnr.bg/horizont/post/101459786/prokuraturata-poiska-5-godini-zatvor-za-bivsh-zam-kmet-na-sofia)

## 4. Kindergartens: places built and children without a place

### Takeaway
Official figures exist only for scattered dates: about 10,000 children unplaced at first ranking in 2020, 2021 and 2022, with the municipality putting the "real" shortage in 2022 at about 7,400 places (1,500 of them for ages 3–6). A year-by-year series for 2010–2023 and an independent count of buildings delivered were not found.

### Cited Findings

- [SELF] As of 19 Oct 2020: 2,281 children short of a kindergarten-group place and 6,644 short of a nursery place. Three-year programme announced: 67 new buildings, 282 groups, about 6,400 places by 2023 (31 buildings/127 groups to start in 2021; 24 buildings/98 groups in 2022–2023); 12 buildings then under construction (read) — [bTV, 21 Oct 2020](https://btvnovinite.bg/bulgaria/67-novi-detski-gradini-v-sofija-do-2023-g.html)

- 2021: about 10,000 children without a place in municipal kindergartens. [DOC, Ministry of Finance data] The government had granted BGN 68 million for kindergartens, BGN 65 million of it for Sofia; at 30 June 2021 74% was unspent, only 11% (BGN 10 million) absorbed, and for 13% no tender had been announced. Fandakova blamed opposition district mayors for slow construction; they denied it (read) — [Mediapool, 9 Jul 2021](https://www.mediapool.bg/stolichnata-obshtina-malchi-pravi-li-targove-samo-s-edin-kandidat-news323964.html)
- [DOC, municipal data] 2022/23 first ranking (12 May 2022): 23,622 applicants (2,559 already attending and seeking a transfer); 13,691 places announced; 10,802 children placed; about 10,261 not admitted. Education directorate head Maria Mincheva: real shortage about 7,400 places, of which 1,500 in kindergarten groups; the rest is nursery age. [SELF] 1,200 new places opened; 20 buildings under construction, 14 due in 2022 (read) — [Sega, 12 May 2022](https://www.segabg.com/category-education/nad-10-200-deca-ostanaha-bez-mesta-detskite-gradini-sofiya); [Webcafe, 12 May 2022](https://webcafe.bg/bulgaria/fandakova-okolo-23-600-detsa-sa-kandidatstvali-za-detska-gradina.html)
- [DOC] The Audit Office audited the municipality's management of kindergartens for 2007–2008 (before her term as mayor, while she was deputy mayor for education); not read — [Audit Office report](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/bg/documents/1789/Doklad-SO-det-gradini-2007-2008-odob1.doc)

### Inferences
- The first-ranking figure (about 10,000) overstates the structural shortage because it includes transfers and nursery-age children; the municipality's own "real shortage" figure for 2022 was about 7,400, mostly nursery places.

## 5. Public transport: vehicles, fares, night transport

### Takeaway
The 2016 fare rise (single ticket BGN 1.00 to 1.60 from 1 June 2016) is documented, as is a first-instance court ruling of 22 Feb 2017 annulling it for lack of economic justification. Night buses ran April 2018 – September 2020 and returned in 2023. Vehicle purchases could only be partly dated, from an enthusiast site.

### Cited Findings
**Fares**
- [DOC] The council decided in April 2016 (Decision 270 of 14 Apr 2016, snippet) to raise the single ticket from BGN 1.00 to 1.60 from June 2016; the same decision doubled the fine for travelling without a ticket to BGN 40 and cut the all-lines annual pass from BGN 500 to 365 (other passes unchanged) (read) — [Sofia Globe, 22 Feb 2017](https://sofiaglobe.com/?p=58732); [Council Decision 270](https://council.sofia.bg/documents/20182/72138/R.+270.pdf) (not opened). The 10-trip card went from BGN 8 to 12; the rise passed with 34 votes (snippet) — [Capital, 14 Apr 2016](https://www.capital.bg/kakvo_stava/2016/04/14/2742320_ednokratniiat_bilet_za_gradskiia_transport_v_sofiia/)
- [DOC] On 22 Feb 2017 the Sofia City Administrative Court annulled the regulation behind the rise: the council gave no specific reasons or price formula for the new ticket price and fine. The ruling was appealable to ВАС and did not immediately change prices (read) — [Sofia Globe, 22 Feb 2017](https://sofiaglobe.com/?p=58732)
- [DOC] The Audit Office found that the Urban Mobility Centre's proposal to raise the price of two ticket types in the 2016 economic framework "contains no information on the mechanism by which the higher price was determined and no analysis of the effect" (read) — [Audit Office report on ЦГМ](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/bg/documents/13485/od-cgm-160517.pdf)
