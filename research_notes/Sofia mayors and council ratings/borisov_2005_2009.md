# Boyko Borisov as Mayor of Sofia (Nov 2005 – Jul 2009) and acting mayor Minko Gerdzhikov (Jul – Nov 2009)

Research date: 2026-11-14. Scope: Sofia Municipality (Столична община) only, 2005–2009.

Reading notes for the report writer:
- Labels used below: **[FACT]** = official result, signed contract, court ruling, institutional press release or official statistic; **[REPORTED]** = stated as fact by a news outlet, not checked against a primary document; **[CLAIM]** = statement by an interested party (mayor, opposition, NGO, company); **[OPINION]** = commentary.
- Capital.bg and the sofia.bg mandate report PDF returned HTTP 403 to my fetches. Anything cited to Capital.bg comes from search-result snippets only, not the full article, and is marked "(snippet only)".
- Several numbers conflict between sources. Conflicts are stated explicitly, not resolved.

## 1. Term dates, backing, election results, acting mayor in 2009

### Takeaway
Borisov won a 2005 partial election as an independent in a low-turnout runoff (about 68% of the vote, turnout about 28%) and was re-elected in the first round on 28 October 2007 as GERB's candidate with about 53.4–53.6% (202,800 votes). He left in late July 2009 to become prime minister; deputy mayor Minko Gerdzhikov (GERB) acted as mayor for roughly four months until Yordanka Fandakova took office on 23 November 2009.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT, secondary source] Took the oath as mayor on 10 November 2005; infobox end date 27 July 2009 (the day he was elected prime minister). — [Bulgarian Wikipedia, Бойко Борисов](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Бойко_Борисов)
- [Conflict] English Wikipedia's list of mayors gives his term as 10 November 2005 – 29 July 2009 and Gerdzhikov's as 29 July – 23 November 2009 (interim, GERB). — [List of mayors of Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mayors_of_Sofia); bg.wikipedia gives 27 July 2009 as the end date — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Бойко_Борисов)
- 2005 partial election: first round 29 October 2005 with 15 candidates; runoff Saturday 5 November 2005 against Tatyana Doncheva (BSP). Gallup exit poll for the runoff: Borisov 67.8%, Doncheva 32.2%. Reported first-round shares: Borisov 38%, Doncheva 23%. Turnout: about 33% in round one, 28% in the runoff. Borisov ran as an independent. — [Novinite, 5 Nov 2005](https://www.novinite.com/articles/54770/Exit+Polls%3A+Independent+Borissov+Elected+Sofia+Mayor+with+68)
- Bulgarian Wikipedia gives the runoff result as 68.5% ("убедително мнозинство (68,5%)"), without vote counts. — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Бойко_Борисов)
- The 2005 vacancy arose from the resignation of Stefan Sofiyanski (mayor 1995–2005). — [Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/view_news.php?id=129005)
- S&P described the autumn-2005 extraordinary mayoral election as having "stabilized" the city's political and economic position. — [Institutional Investor, 9 Feb 2006](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2btfn8xopmulkw6t13e9s/innovation/bulgaria-s-p-improves-outlook-for-city-of-sofia)
- 2007 regular election, 28 October 2007, won in the first round as the GERB candidate. "Final results" per Novinite: Borisov 53.43% (202,800 votes); Martin Zaimov (joint right-wing candidate) 17.77% (67,454); Brigo Asparuhov (BSP) 15.48% (58,774); Slavi Binev (Ataka) 4.02%; Antonia Parvanova (NMSP) 2.55%. — [Novinite, 29 Oct 2007](https://www.novinite.com/articles/86963/Sofia+Mayor+Secures+Re-election+with+53.43+-+Final+Results)
- [Conflict] Bulgarian Wikipedia gives 53.58% for the 2007 first round, and says GERB candidates also won a majority in the Sofia City Council. — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Бойко_Борисов). The Novinite article of 29 Oct 2007 said only that GERB was "poised to win a plurality of seats". — [Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/86963/Sofia+Mayor+Secures+Re-election+with+53.43+-+Final+Results)
- GERB was founded on 3 December 2006, i.e. during his first (independent) term. — [Bulgarian Wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/Бойко_Борисов)
- Zaimov was the joint SDS–DSB candidate and finished second ahead of BSP's Asparuhov. — [Capital, 4 Sep 2009 (snippet only)](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2009/09/04/779491_v_siankata_na_boiko_borisov/)
- Acting mayor: Minko Gerdzhikov signed an EIB loan on 22 October 2009 styled "acting Mayor of Sofia", with Prime Minister Borisov present. — [EIB press release 2009-208](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2009-208-eib-boosts-infrastructure-and-small-companies-in-bulgaria)
- Fandakova was elected on 15 November 2009 and took office on 23 November 2009; Gerdzhikov is listed as her (acting) predecessor. — [Yordanka Fandakova, English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yordanka_Fandakova)
- Gerdzhikov afterwards served as deputy mayor for finance and resigned in March 2011 over a property-tax scandal (outside the period). — [Novinite](https://www.novinite.com/articles/126167/Sofia+Deputy+Mayor+Resigns+over+Property+Tax+Scandal)

### Inferences
- Acting period was about 117–119 days (27 or 29 July to 23 November 2009), depending on which end date is right.
- The 67.8% (exit poll) and 68.5% (Wikipedia) figures are close enough that "about 68%" is safe; the exact official figure is not confirmed.
- The 38%/23% first-round figures for 2005 were published on runoff night and may be rounded or preliminary.
- The 53.43% vs 53.58% gap for 2007 probably reflects different counting stages; neither was checked against the Central Election Commission.

### Gaps
- No Central Election Commission (cik.bg) primary data retrieved for either election: official vote counts for 2005 (both rounds), official 2007 turnout in Sofia, and the 2007 council seat distribution are all unverified.
- Exact date and vote by which the Sofia City Council chose Gerdzhikov as acting mayor were not found.
- Which parties formally endorsed Borisov's 2005 independent candidacy (nomination committee vs party support) was not established.

## 2. Concrete achievements (metro, roads, EU funds, finances, transport, kindergartens)

### Takeaway
The best-documented positives are the metro: 8.0 km and 6 stations of Line 1 were opened in May and September 2009 (works started 2005–2006, with a roughly two-year delay on the central section), Line 2 contracts were signed in August 2008 and a EUR 105 million EIB loan, the bank's first direct loan to a Bulgarian municipality, was signed in November 2008. Evidence on roads, public transport fleet, kindergartens and the credit rating during the term is thin; S&P held Sofia at BB with a positive outlook in February 2006.

### Inferences
- Line 1 sections opened in 2009 total about 8.0 km and 6 stations (4.8 km/3 stations + 3.2 km/3 stations per Metropolitan). The 5.7 km/5 stations (May) figure from Wikipedia implies about 2.3 km and 1 new station (Sofia University) for the September link; the two sources slice the sections differently but the totals agree.
- Work on the central section began in 2005 under a JBIC loan arranged before Borisov's term, so he inherited the project and its financing; what is attributable to his administration is execution (with a roughly two-year delay), the Interpred – Mladost 1 section started in 2006, and the Line 2 contracts and EIB loan in 2008.
- The EIB "first direct loan to a Bulgarian municipality" is an external signal of creditworthiness in late 2008, consistent with S&P's positive outlook in 2006.
- The dog population appears to have fallen between 2006 and 2007, but counts come from different methods and the sources are secondary; treat the trend as indicative only.

### Gaps
- S&P rating actions for Sofia between 2007 and 2009 were not found; the rating level at the end of the term is unverified.
- No sourced figures found for buses, trolleybuses or trams bought in 2005–2009, nor for kindergartens built or places added.
- EU grant amounts actually secured or contracted for Sofia during the term (as opposed to later OP Transport funding) were not found.
- The mayor's own mandate report for 2007–2011 exists at [sofia.bg](https://www.sofia.bg/documents/58109/1591890/%D0%9E%D1%82%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%82+2007-2011.pdf/e0a3bf80-b595-41a9-bf39-6621245c548a) but returned HTTP 403; it would be the municipality's own account of roads, transport and kindergartens and should be read manually.
- No verified list of streets, boulevards or overpasses completed in 2006–2009 was found.

## 3. Documented failures and scandals

### Takeaway
Waste is the clearest documented failure: Sofia had no compliant disposal facility throughout the term, the promised treatment plant was not built (it opened in 2015), baled waste stockpiles grew, the old Suhodol landfill was reopened in December 2007, the European Commission opened an infringement procedure in October 2007 and referred Bulgaria to the Court of Justice in November 2009, and a contractor stoppage in March 2009 left the city uncleaned until the government stepped in. The problem was inherited (Suhodol blockade July 2005, bales from the Sofiyanski era), and responsibility was disputed between the municipality and the BSP-led government. On Toplofikatsiya the record is partly to his credit (the director was removed in April 2006 and later convicted); on Sofiyski imoti, the urban plan, snow clearing and procurement I found mostly reported criticism, not rulings against his administration.

### Inferences
- The waste failure has three separable layers: (a) inherited (no compliant landfill, Suhodol blockade July 2005, concessions signed in the late 1990s, bales already stockpiled); (b) on his watch (no plant started despite repeated deadlines, bale stockpile growth, EU infringement opened ten months after accession, 2009 service stoppage); (c) shared with the central government (environmental permits, emergency funding). A rating should weigh (b) but not attribute (a).
- Bale figures are in different units and cannot be compared directly: 160,000 tonnes (Borisov's claim of inheritance, 2006), nearly 300,000 tonnes (Mediapool, Sept 2006), about 600,000 bales (Capital, Aug 2008). They do indicate growth during 2006–2008.
- The April state of emergency is almost certainly April 2009 (it follows the March 2009 stoppage and the natural-disaster-fund payments), though Novinite gives no year.
- Toplofikatsiya: the offences date from Dimitrov's 2002–2006 tenure, mostly before Borisov. The dismissal in April 2006, five months into the term, counts in Borisov's favour; the company's debt crisis in 2008 with the municipality as 58% owner counts against the owners jointly. Note the tension that Capital credits Borisov's audit while the January 2007 replacement director was elected by opposition councillors.
- Sofiyski imoti: the charged deals found (2003 sales, park in-kind contributions) predate Borisov. I found no evidence tying his administration to them, nor evidence of what he did about the company.
- The urban plan was adopted by parliament as a law (late 2006) on the municipality's proposal; criticism documented here is the Ombudsman's concern over green areas, which is institutional but not a finding of illegality.

- No court rulings or audit findings found on snow-clearing or on procurement by the municipality in this period; evidence is press reporting and opposition claims only.

## 4. Findings from the National Audit Office (Сметна палата) and other audits

### Takeaway
I found no National Audit Office report specifically on Sofia Municipality's management in 2005–2009. The audits located concern earlier periods (the Sofiyska Voda water concession and metro construction, published 2005; municipal real estate 1998–2005), so they bear mainly on the predecessor.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT, audit institution] In an interview dated October 2005, National Audit Office president Valeri Dimitrov said the report on the Sofia water concession, published together with an audit of metro construction, was unfavourable to the municipality: during the four audited years the mayor had not submitted a single report to the council on the concessionaire's performance, contrary to the Municipal Property Act and the municipal ordinance. — [Сметна палата, interview for Dnevnik, 11 Oct 2005](https://bulnao.government.bg/bg/documents/12003/interview-dnevnik111005.doc) (the audited period precedes Borisov's term)
- [FACT, audit institution] The Office's 2006 programme audited management and disposal of private municipal property in nineteen municipalities, including Sofia, for 1998–2005. — [Сметна палата, summary report on municipalities](https://bulnao.government.bg/bg/documents/149/Obobshten-obshtini08.doc)
- [REPORTED] An inspection ordered by the municipality at Toplofikatsiya in 2006 led to the director's dismissal and a prosecution (see section 3). — [Capital, 25 Jan 2013 (snippet only)](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2013/01/25/1991149_erata_na_toploto/)

### Inferences
- The audit trail found documents weaknesses Borisov inherited (unsupervised water concession, property disposals up to 2005) and gives no direct institutional verdict on his own administration.

### Gaps
- Not found: any Сметна палата audit of Sofia Municipality's budget, waste fee, procurement or municipal companies covering 2006–2009; any State Financial Inspection Agency (АДФИ) findings for those years. The bulnao.government.bg archive was not searched exhaustively and the two documents above were seen via search summaries, not read in full.
- Whether the 1998–2005 property audit contains findings touching November–December 2005 is unknown.

## 5. Municipal budget and debt at the start and end of the period

### Takeaway
Hard numbers are sparse. At the start, S&P rated Sofia BB with a positive outlook (February 2006) and expected debt to climb towards 70% of annual revenue; at the end, the municipality entered 2010 with a reported deficit of about BGN 12 million on a budget of over BGN 1.2 billion, and had added EUR 105 million (2008) and EUR 43.5 million (October 2009) in EIB borrowing.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] February 2006: rating BB, outlook raised to positive; debt expected to rise to 70% of annual revenues for transport infrastructure; S&P noted Sofia generates about 25% of national GDP and attracts about 60% of FDI but keeps limited investment resources because tax receipts are redistributed. — [Institutional Investor, 9 Feb 2006](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2btfn8xopmulkw6t13e9s/innovation/bulgaria-s-p-improves-outlook-for-city-of-sofia)
- [REPORTED] Waste fee revenue about BGN 100 million a year in 2006. — [Mediapool, 11 Sep 2006](https://www.mediapool.bg/kmetat-borisov-boklukat-na-sofiya-ne-e-moi-news121151.html)
- [FACT] New borrowing: EIB EUR 105 million for the metro, 21 November 2008. — [EIB press release 2008-114](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2008-114-eib-supports-development-of-public-transport-in-sofia)
- [FACT] New borrowing: EIB EUR 43.5 million for road junctions, signed 22 October 2009 by acting mayor Gerdzhikov. — [EIB press release 2009-208](https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2009-208-eib-boosts-infrastructure-and-small-companies-in-bulgaria)
- [FACT] Earlier JBIC loan to the municipality financed the Sv. Nedelya – Vasil Levski Stadium metro section (amount not stated). — [Metropolitan EAD](https://www.metropolitan.bg/en/op-transport-extension/istoriya-razvitie-metro)
- [REPORTED] Draft 2010 budget presented by deputy mayor Gerdzhikov: over BGN 1.2 billion, about BGN 200 million more than the previous year; the municipality entered 2010 with a deficit of about BGN 12 million; property-sale and tax revenues expected to fall because of the crisis. — [Capital, 22 Jan 2010 (snippet only)](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2010/01/22/846712_picata_na_sofiia/)
- [REPORTED] The Sofia City Council adopted the 2009 budget on 19 February 2009. — [Novinite, 16 Mar 2009](https://m.novinite.com/view_news.php?id=101967)
- [REPORTED] Unpaid bills to the cleaning contractor of over BGN 10 million in February 2009. — [Mediapool, 8 Oct 2025](https://www.mediapool.bg/boiko-i-valka-posledniya-pat-kogato-v-sofiya-beshe-rezhisirana-kriza-s-bokluka-kmetat-stana-premier-news375967.html)
- [REPORTED, acting period] Under Gerdzhikov as acting mayor, the municipality announced local taxes would not be increased (headline only). — [Novinite](https://m.novinite.com/view_news.php?id=108930)

### Inferences
- "About BGN 200 million more than the previous year" implies a 2009 budget of roughly BGN 1.0 billion.
- Debt rose during the term in absolute terms (at least EUR 148.5 million of new EIB commitments signed 2008–2009, not necessarily all drawn), in line with S&P's 2006 expectation; whether the debt-to-revenue ratio rose or fell cannot be determined from what I found.
- Spending on cleaning roughly doubled over the term if the Mediapool 2025 series (40 to 80 million) is right, but that series conflicts with Mediapool's 2006 figure for 2005 (see section 3).

### Gaps
- Not found: total municipal debt at end-2005 and at end-2009; annual budget totals and execution for 2006, 2007, 2008; cash balances or arrears at handover; S&P rating actions for 2007–2009.
- Best primary sources to consult manually: Ministry of Finance municipal debt register, Sofia City Council budget execution reports for 2005 and 2009, and S&P's rating history for the City of Sofia.
- Almost nothing was found on Gerdzhikov's own decisions as acting mayor beyond the EIB loan signing and the metro link opening in September 2009.
