# Vasil Terziev as Mayor of Sofia (Столична община), November 2023 – November 2026

Research date: 14 November 2026. **The term is unfinished** (regular local elections are due in autumn 2027), so every assessment below is interim. Many projects cited as achievements are still under construction or only financed; several court cases (election challenge, parking reform, the Barbutov case) are unresolved.

Labels used in the findings:
- **[FACT]** – court ruling, council vote, official result, signed contract or an event reported consistently by several independent outlets.
- **[SELF-REPORTED]** – figure that originates from the municipality or the mayor and that I could not check against an independent source.
- **[ALLEGATION]** / **[OPINION]** – claim by a party to a dispute, or commentary.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** – sources conflict, or I only saw a search snippet and not the full text.

Caveat on method: several key pages (Capital.bg, results.cik.bg, bgonair.bg) returned HTTP 403 to my fetches, so for those I relied on search-result summaries. Those items are marked.

## 1. Election 2023: coalition, results, margin, court challenges

### Takeaway
Terziev (nominated by PP-DB-Spasi Sofia) won the 5 November 2023 runoff against Vanya Grigorova by 4,786 votes (48.2% vs 46.9%), the narrowest win in Sofia's recent history, on low turnout. Grigorova's legal challenge is still not finally resolved three years on: the first-instance court upheld the result in July 2024, but the Supreme Administrative Court sent the case back for retrial in February 2025.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Runoff, 5 Nov 2023: Terziev 48.2%, Grigorova (backed by BSP and "Levitsata") 46.9%, "I do not support anyone" 4.9%; margin 4,786 votes, described as the narrowest in Sofia's recent history — [Sofia Globe, 6 Nov 2023](https://sofiaglobe.com/2023/11/06/bulgarias-2023-mayoral-elections-vassil-terziev-elected-mayor-of-sofia/)
- [FACT] Vote counts: Terziev 175,044 (48.20%), Grigorova 170,258 (46.89%) — [E-News.bg](https://e-news.bg/vas-deloto-za-inborn-na-kmet-na-sofia-zapotsva-otnatsalo/). (175,044 − 170,258 = 4,786, consistent with the margin above.)
- [FACT] Council seats (61 total) from the first round: PP-DB-Spasi Sofia 23, GERB-SDS 14, BSP coalition 9; the rest split among smaller groups — [Sofia Globe](https://sofiaglobe.com/2023/11/06/bulgarias-2023-mayoral-elections-vassil-terziev-elected-mayor-of-sofia/). So Terziev's own coalition held 23 of 61 seats, 8 short of a majority.
- [FACT] Turnout at 16:00 on runoff day was 23.7% versus 25.6% at the same hour in round one — [Sofia Globe](https://sofiaglobe.com/2023/11/06/bulgarias-2023-mayoral-elections-vassil-terziev-elected-mayor-of-sofia/)
- [UNVERIFIED] Final runoff turnout "around 34%"; first round: Terziev "about 37%", Grigorova 20.7%, Hekimyan (GERB) 18.9% — [KInsights/Capital, 30 Oct 2023](https://kinsights.capital.bg/politics_and_society/2023/10/30/4545917_local_elections_round_1_gerb_loses_sofia_keeps_burgas/) and [KInsights, 6 Nov 2023](https://kinsights.capital.bg/politics_and_society/2023/11/06/4549542_the_day_in_3_news_terziev_wins_narrowly_in_sofia_for/). These look like exit-poll or early-count numbers, not the final CIK protocol; treat as approximate.
- [OPINION/analysis] Capital described "two Sofias": Grigorova stronger in northern districts and the periphery, Terziev in central and southern districts — [Capital, 8 Nov 2023](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2023/11/08/4550472_mestniiat_vot_razkri_dve_sofii_karta/)
- [FACT] Court challenge: in July 2024 the Sofia City Administrative Court upheld the Municipal Election Commission decision of 6 Nov 2023 declaring Terziev elected. On 7 Feb 2025 the Supreme Administrative Court (VAS) annulled that ruling for procedural violations and returned the case to a different panel of the lower court; the VAS ruling itself is final, but the case restarts — [BTA, Feb 2025](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/829552-supreme-administrative-court-returns-sofia-mayor-election-case-for-new-considera); [Investor.bg](https://www.investor.bg/a/516-politika/408398-vas-varna-za-prerazglezhdane-deloto-za-izbora-na-kmet-na-sofiya); [RFE/RL Bulgaria](https://bulgaria.rferl.org/a/terziev-sofiya-obzhalvane/33307108.html)
- [OPINION] Sega commented at the time that the case would probably not finish before the end of the term — [Sega](https://www.segabg.com/hot/category-bulgaria/vas-otmeni-uzakonyavaneto-na-izbora-na-terziev)
- [FACT, date not confirmed] The anti-corruption commission found no violations in Terziev's shareholding in a joint-stock company (an incompatibility complaint against him) — [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/bulgaria/1017513-anti-corruption-commission-finds-no-violations-in-sofia-mayor-terziev-s-sharehol)

### Inferences
- The election has never been annulled; Terziev has governed with full legal authority throughout, but under a pending challenge for the entire three years.
- A mandate of roughly 175,000 votes in a city of over 1.2 million, with 23 of 61 council seats, explains much of the governing difficulty described in section 2.

### Gaps
- Official CIK first-round figures (votes, %, turnout) and final runoff turnout: results.cik.bg returned HTTP 403. The writer should take these from results.cik.bg/mi2023 directly.
- Status of the retrial in the Sofia City Administrative Court after Feb 2025: I found no report of a new first-instance ruling in 2025–2026.

## 2. Relationship with the Sofia Municipal Council (СОС)

### Takeaway
Terziev has never had a council majority. The council needed nearly three months and eight attempts to elect a chair (Feb 2024), the 2025 budget was adopted only on 26 June 2025 after a mayoral return and a veto override on transport pay, Spasi Sofia left the coalition in April 2025, a council majority replaced the boards of six key municipal companies against his will in July 2024, and ten deputy mayors have left in three years. The 2024 and 2026 budgets, by contrast, passed with broad majorities.

### Cited Findings
**Council chair**
- [FACT] After a first sitting that lasted almost three months and seven failed attempts, the council elected Tsvetomir Petrov (PP-DB-SS) as chair on the eighth attempt in February 2024, initially on a temporary basis until the summer recess. Boris Bonev (Spasi Sofia) had been the coalition's first candidate and failed to get support. The ballot was secret; Capital wrote that Petrov evidently received votes from GERB, BSP, Vazrazhdane and "Sinya Sofia" — [Capital, 8 Feb 2024](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2024/02/08/4585892_stolichniiat_obshtinski_suvet_nai-posle_si_izbra/)
- [FACT] Petrov said the council's real work began only nine months before the first anniversary because of the fragmentation; 566 decisions were adopted in the first year — [BTA video](https://www.bta.bg/bg/videos/23887)
- [FACT] GERB-SDS councillors later demanded Petrov's resignation — [BTA video](https://www.bta.bg/bg/videos/24033)

**Budgets**
- [FACT] 2024 budget adopted 28 March 2024 (Decision No. 49), 45 for, 15 against; about 2.576bn leva versus 2.564bn in 2023, presented by the mayor as a record — [Council decision](https://council.sofia.bg/documents/d/guest/r-49-2024); [Investor.bg](https://www.investor.bg/a/540-danatsi-i-byudzhet/391654-stolichniyat-obshtinski-savet-prie-rekorden-byudzhet-na-sofiya-za-2024-g); [Mediapool](https://www.mediapool.bg/byudzhetat-na-sofiya-ot-26-mlrd-leva-beshe-priet-vapreki-kritikite-news357618.html)
- [FACT] 2025 budget: first voted 29 May 2025; Terziev returned it for reconsideration as unlawful and inexpedient; finally adopted 26 June 2025 (Decision No. 475, promulgated 3 July 2025) with 41 votes, about 2.8bn leva — [Sofia Municipality budget page](https://www.sofia.bg/en/2025-financial-year); [RFE/RL Bulgaria](https://www.svobodnaevropa.bg/a/obshtinski-savet-byudzhet-sofiya-zaplati-transport/33455544.html); [Club Z](https://clubz.bg/163130); [Investor.bg](https://www.investor.bg/a/540-danatsi-i-byudzhet/415474-sofiya-veche-ima-priet-byudzhet-za-2025-g). Outlets differ on the exact total (2.8bn, "over 2.84bn", 2.932bn for the May version) — [Krasiva Sofia](https://krasivasofia.bg/News/Home/Details/181).
- [FACT] 2026 budget adopted 24 September 2026, 49 for, 6 against, 3 abstentions; about EUR 1.543bn; capital programme about EUR 300m. The delay was attributed to the late state budget; the council had earlier approved a capital programme and a temporary financial framework — [Capital, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/ikonomika/2026/09/24/4958847_sofiia_oficialno_prie_bjudjeta_si_za_2026_g_-_s_300/); [Euronews Bulgaria](https://euronews.bg/news/sos-prie-byudzheta-na-sofiya-za-2026-g-za-154-mlrd-evro/); [Club Z](https://clubz.bg/179787)
- [FACT] The council approved an investment programme financed by up to EUR 367m of new debt; the Vazrazhdane group voted against — [BTA council feed](https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/bulgaria/municipal-council/sofia-grad); draft decision with max. municipal debt of EUR 476.2m at end-2026 — [council draft](https://council.sofia.bg/documents/d/guest/soa26-vk66-7773-terziev) (draft figures, not checked against the final text)

**Vetoes / returned decisions**
- [FACT] After the May 2025 strike the council voted a 300-leva rise in base pay for all roughly 7,000 staff of the four transport companies, backdated to 1 April. Terziev vetoed the 29 May decision; the council re-adopted it 35 for, 19 against, 4 abstentions — [Economic.bg](https://www.economic.bg/bg/a/view/obshtinarite-v-sofija-odobriha-300-lv-uvelichenie-na-zaplatite-v-gradskija-transport); [bTV](https://btvnovinite.bg/bulgaria/stolichnijat-obshtinski-savet-sos-odobri-uvelichenieto-s-300-lv-na-zaplatite-v-nazemnija-gradski-transport.html); [OFFNews](https://offnews.bg/obshtestvo/sos-glasuva-zaplatite-v-gradskia-transport-da-se-vdignat-s-300-lv-st-846883.html)

**Split with Spasi Sofia**
- [FACT] On 22 April 2025 Boris Bonev announced that Spasi Sofia was leaving the coalition with PP and DB and withdrawing confidence from the mayor; its 8 councillors formed a separate group; its only deputy mayor, Iliyan Pavlov (transport), resigned — [Mediapool](https://www.mediapool.bg/bulgaria/spasi-sofiya-napusna-koalitsiyata-s-vasil-terziev/); [bTV](https://btvnovinite.bg/bulgaria/spasi-sofija-napuska-koalicijata-s-pp-i-db-svalja-doverieto-si-ot-kmeta-vasil-terziev.html)
- [ALLEGATION, Bonev] Reasons given: breach of the coalition agreement, lack of results and reforms, and an informal "parallel municipality" of consultants and campaign staff around the mayor — [Epicenter](https://epicenter.bg/article/-quot-Spasi-Sofiya-quot--Svalyame-doverieto-si-ot-kmeta-Vasil-Terziev/384684/2/0); context in [Banker](https://banker.bg/2025/04/23/spasi-sofiya-izostavi-vasil-terziev/)
- [FACT] Spasi Sofia later demanded the resignation of the deputy mayor for construction and asked the mayor to declare a state of emergency in five districts over waste — [BTA video](https://www.bta.bg/bg/videos/31707); still, in 2026 it said it would back the budget if its proposals stayed in — [BTA council feed](https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/bulgaria/municipal-council/sofia-grad)

**Municipal company boards**
- [FACT] In July 2024 a council majority (reported as GERB, BSP and ITN) voted a report launching replacement of the managements of six of the largest municipal companies; the long-serving metro chief Stoyan Bratoev was among those removed. Terziev called it "an ugly play" and Bratoev's removal "an attack on Sofia residents", and objected that councillors had a 120-page report for under 24 hours — [Sega](https://www.segabg.com/hot/category-bulgaria/gerb-bsp-i-itn-smenyat-shefovete-na-klyuchovi-firmi-na-sofiya); [Lupa.bg](https://lupa.bg/news/terziev-izhvarlyaneto-na-stoyan-bratoev-ot-quotmetropolitenquot-e-atentat-sreshtu-sofiyanci_303040news.html)
- [SELF-REPORTED admission] The municipality's own three-year report says: "We did not manage to prevail in the dispute over the managements of key municipal companies" (report p. 4) — [SO report](https://www.sofia.bg/documents/d/guest/otchet-so-november-2023-2026)

**Deputy mayors**
- [FACT] Ten deputy mayors left in three years (count as of 1 Oct 2026): Ivan Matov (construction, Apr 2024, "personal reasons"); Yana Genova (culture, early 2025, said she lacked the trust needed); Iliyan Pavlov (transport, Apr 2025, after Spasi Sofia's exit); Nikola Barbutov (June 2025, dismissed after his detention); Ivan Vasilev (finance, about July 2025, resigned); Nadezhda Bobcheva (ecology, Mar 2026); Blagorodna Zdravkova (culture and tourism, 2026, retirement); Nikola Lyutov (construction, about 29 Sep 2026, "mutual agreement"); Viktor Chaushev (transport, 1 Oct 2026); Ivan Goychev (digitalisation, 1 Oct 2026, remains a councillor) — [Club Z, 1 Oct 2026](https://clubz.bg/180022); [Mediapool](https://www.mediapool.bg/bulgaria/vasil-terziev-pak-smeni-zam-kmet-10-i-ot-nachaloto-na-mandata-obnovena/)
- [FACT] Club Z notes that the "12 departures" figure used by Spasi Sofia also counts chief architects and people who were never deputy mayors. Georgi Klisurski left temporarily in 2026 to be caretaker finance minister and returned in May — [Club Z](https://clubz.bg/180022)
- [UNVERIFIED] Media linked Vasilev's resignation to the dispute over changing the municipality's servicing bank (Municipal Bank); he did not state his reasons publicly — [Mediapool](https://www.mediapool.bg/bulgaria/vasil-terziev-pak-smeni-zam-kmet-10-i-ot-nachaloto-na-mandata-obnovena/)
- [FACT] A new transport deputy (Ivan Nikolov) was then appointed with a plan for nearly 400 new vehicles — [Boulevard Bulgaria](https://boulevardbulgaria.bg/articles/ivan-nikolov-poema-transporta-na-sofiya-s-plan-za-blizo-400-novi-avtobusa-i-tramvaya)

### Inferences
- Budget timing is mixed, not uniformly bad: 2024 (end of March) was normal for a year with a late state budget; 2025 (end of June, after a return) was a real governance failure shared between mayor and council; 2026 (September) was late mainly because of the national budget, and passed 49–6.
- Three transport deputies in three years (Pavlov, Chaushev, then Nikolov) and three construction deputies (Matov, Lyutov, successor) indicate instability in exactly the portfolios where delivery matters most.
- The loss of Spasi Sofia cut the mayor's reliable base from 23 to about 15 of 61 seats.

### Gaps
- Exact dates and votes for each failed chair ballot; whether Petrov is still chair in November 2026.
- A full list of mayoral returns/vetoes and how many were overridden; council.sofia.bg would be the source.
- Who replaced each departed deputy mayor and the current line-up.

## 3. Concrete achievements (independently reported)

### Takeaway
The most tangible deliveries are three new metro stations (August 2026), nearly 3,000 new kindergarten/nursery places with a 73% cut in the first-group shortfall, several completed road links, a confirmed investment-grade credit rating, open-data and transparency tools, and measured winter air-quality improvement. Most of the large transport renewal (345 vehicles, seven more metro stations, Copenhagen Blvd.) is financed or contracted but not yet delivered.

### Cited Findings
**Metro**
- [FACT] Three stations of Line 3 (Stadion Georgi Asparuhov, Besarabia, Gen. Vladimir Vazov), about 3 km from Hadzhi Dimitar to Levski G, opened to passengers on 14–15 August 2026; cost over EUR 144m, financed through the National Recovery and Resilience Plan; network now about 55 km — [Investor.bg](https://www.investor.bg/a/523-transport-i-infrastruktura/435097-tri-novi-stantsii-byaha-otkriti-po-tretata-liniya-na-stolichnoto-metro); [BNT](https://bntnews.bg/news/noviyat-uchastak-na-metroto-liniya-3-veche-stigna-do-kvartal-levski-g-1408076news.html); [Gradat.bg](https://gradat.bg/infrastruktura-i-gradska-sreda/metroto-stigna-do-levski-g-tri-novi-stancii-ot-liniya-3-veche)
- [FACT] Work continues on six stations towards Slatina/Tsarigradsko Shose; four more trains ordered for Line 3 — [Novinite.bg](https://m.novinite.bg/articles/288909/tri-novi-metrostancii-otvaryat-vrati-v-sofiya)

**Kindergartens and schools**
- [SELF-REPORTED, widely relayed] Nearly 3,000 new kindergarten/nursery places; first-group shortfall down from 1,373 (2023) to 372 (2026); EUR 122.8m invested in educational infrastructure 2024–2026; new municipal school in Manastirski Livadi-West opened September 2026 — [Stolica.bg, 13 Nov 2026](https://stolica.bg/stolichna-obshtina/vasil-terziev-otchete-tri-godini-upravlenie-na-sofiya-kakvo-e-postignato-i-kakvo-predstoi); [Eurocom](https://eurocom.bg/2026/11/13/terziev-otchete-3-godini-upravlenie-sofiya-navaksva-v-detski-gradini-transport-i-zeleni-ploshti)

**Roads**
- [SELF-REPORTED, relayed by media] Completed: Todor Kableshkov Blvd. breakthrough, extension of Rozhen Blvd. to the Northern Speed Tangent, overpass at Bakarena Fabrika; started: Copenhagen Blvd. link between Druzhba and Mladost — [Stolica.bg](https://stolica.bg/stolichna-obshtina/vasil-terziev-otchete-tri-godini-upravlenie-na-sofiya-kakvo-e-postignato-i-kakvo-predstoi)

**Public transport fleet**
- [FACT, plans/contracts not deliveries] Plan for 345 vehicles (200 buses, 75 trolleybuses, 50 e-buses, 20 trams), with EUR 265m EU funding for 145 of them — [Blitz](https://blitz.bg/stolitsa/sofiya-obnovyava-gradskiya-transport-s-345-novi-prevozni-sredstva_news1166445.html); e-buses due within 9 months and trolleybuses within 12–15 months of financing — [News.bg](https://news.bg/regions/sofiya-zapochva-mashtabno-obnovyavane-na-tramvai-trolei-i-elektrobusi.html)
- [FACT] 25 second-hand articulated Mercedes CapaCity buses from Vienna to enter service by end-2026; 42 second-hand articulated buses in 2026–2027; tenders for 200 new buses to start early 2027 with delivery in 2028 — [DBR.bg](https://dbr.bg/sofia-puska-25-novi-avtobusa-mercedes-po-nai-natovarenite-linii-do-kraa-na-godinata~26006.html); [Boulevard Bulgaria](https://boulevardbulgaria.bg/articles/ivan-nikolov-poema-transporta-na-sofiya-s-plan-za-blizo-400-novi-avtobusa-i-tramvaya)
- [FACT] Route-network changes (24 changes after new bus routes) — [Sofia Municipality](https://www.sofia.bg/en/w/7214100)

**Finances**
- [FACT] S&P Global Ratings affirmed Sofia's long-term rating at BBB on 23 October 2025, equal to the sovereign at the time (a municipality cannot be rated above its sovereign) — [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/866906-s-p-rating-agency-confirms-bbb-credit-rating-for-sofia-municipality)
- [FACT] Record nominal budgets each year: 2.576bn leva (2024), about 2.8bn leva (2025), EUR 1.543bn (2026) — sources in section 2
- [FACT] Finance minister cited a municipal surplus of 638m leva as of 31 March 2025 during the strike dispute, arguing the city could fund pay rises itself — [BTA Media Review, 21 May 2025](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/895189-media-review-may-21)
- [SELF-REPORTED via Capital headline] In September 2026 "almost all" capital-programme projects were under way; the finance deputy said a small part had no contractor — [Capital](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/ikonomika/2026/09/24/4958847_sofiia_oficialno_prie_bjudjeta_si_za_2026_g_-_s_300/)

**Air quality**
- [SELF-REPORTED from official monitoring] Winter 2025/26 vs previous winter: PM10 over 24% lower, NO2 over 15% lower, exceedance days over 60% fewer; Sofia met EU PM10 limits three years running — [Forbes Bulgaria, 20 Oct 2026](https://forbesbulgaria.com/2026/10/20/niskoemisionnata-zona-v-sofiya-mozhe-da-stane-tselogodishna/); [Capital, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.capital.bg/zelen-kapital/2026/10/21/4969292_sofiia_-_mejdu_zaprashavaneto_i_zonite_s_niski_emisii/)
- [FACT] Low-emission zone for cars introduced from December 2024; winter extension to the "big ring" 1 Dec 2025 – 28 Feb 2026 — [sofiabezemisii.bg](https://sofiabezemisii.bg/low-emission-zones-automobiles/); [Sofia Municipality](https://www.sofia.bg/en/w/6878228)
- [OPINION/analysis] Capital notes about 80% of winter PM exceedances come from solid-fuel household heating and that the heating zone is at an early stage of enforcement — [Capital](https://www.capital.bg/zelen-kapital/2026/10/21/4969292_sofiia_-_mejdu_zaprashavaneto_i_zonite_s_niski_emisii/)

**Waste prices (the positive side of the crisis)**
- [FACT] Zone 3 (Slatina, Poduyane, Izgrev): five-year contract with the "Chista Sofia" consortium at EUR 134 per tonne of household waste; VAS finally upheld the award on 20 October 2026 (case 8320/2026) — [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/bulgaria/1230044-vas-okonchatelno-potvardi-izbora-na-izpalnitel-na-deynostite-po-chistotata-v-ray); [Investor.bg](https://www.investor.bg/a/523-transport-i-infrastruktura/438387-vas-okonchatelno-potvardi-izbora-na-izpalnitel-za-pochistvaneto-na-slatina-poduyane-i-izgrev). Compare with the roughly 400–420 leva (about EUR 205–215) per tonne offered in the Lyulin/Krasno Selo zone in 2025.

**Other**
- [FACT] Agreement with the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences on 82 decares for Kukuryak Park after more than 16 years of dispute; municipality took over the Vrana park-museum — [Stolica.bg](https://stolica.bg/stolichna-obshtina/vasil-terziev-otchete-tri-godini-upravlenie-na-sofiya-kakvo-e-postignato-i-kakvo-predstoi)
- [FACT] Temporary pedestrianisation of a central boulevard in summer 2026; a municipal on-site survey (97 interviews, explicitly not representative) showed 90.7% support — [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/local-focus/1197428-sofia-initiative-for-more-pedestrian-areas-in-summer-gets-high-approval-in-surve)

### Inferences
- The Line 3 extension to Levski G was planned, financed and largely contracted before November 2023 (the RRP funding predates the term); Terziev's administration completed and opened it. I did not find a source apportioning credit, so the writer should describe it as "opened under" rather than "initiated by" him.
- The transport-fleet renewal is the biggest potential achievement but, as of November 2026, almost no new vehicles have entered service; the only near-term additions are second-hand buses.
- The EUR 134/tonne Zone 3 price supports the mayor's argument that the 2025 offers were inflated, though it was reached after an eight-month service disruption.

### Gaps
- Capital-programme execution rates per year (planned vs spent) – not found in any source; the municipality's report omits them too. The annual budget execution reports on sofia.bg or the National Audit Office would be needed.
- Whether S&P or Fitch issued a 2026 action on Sofia specifically (the sovereign was upgraded to BBB+ after euro adoption) – not found.
- Independent verification of kindergarten places and of the air-quality figures (e.g. ExEA/EEA data).
- Procurement-reform metrics (share of single-bid tenders, in-house awards) – nothing found.

## 4. Documented failures and controversies

### Takeaway
The gravest failures are the 2025–2026 waste-collection breakdown (about a month in Lyulin and Krasno Selo, about eight months in Poduyane, Slatina and Izgrev, by the municipality's own account), the six-day surface-transport strike of May 2025, the arrest of a deputy mayor on bribery charges in June 2025 (the court case was sent back to the prosecution in March 2026, no verdict), a parking reform suspended by the courts, a traffic reorganisation the mayor admits was mishandled, an unresolved crisis at Toplofikatsiya Sofia (debts above EUR 1.3bn), and very high turnover among deputy mayors.

### Cited Findings
**Waste-collection crisis 2025–2026**
- [FACT] The contract for Lyulin and Krasno Selo with the incumbent (Zauba) expired on 4 October 2025. The municipal company Sofekostroy refused to take over (a few trucks against a need for over 50). The municipality refused to sign with the only remaining bidder, a consortium around "Trans 2025", whose price was over 400 leva per tonne against 144 leva paid until then — [Mediapool, 4 Oct 2025](https://www.mediapool.bg/terziev-sofiya-nyama-da-se-ogane-pred-reketa-za-bokluka-nyama-da-plashtame-taksa-koruptsiya-news375909.html)

### Inferences
- Several headline numbers are stated inconsistently inside the same document (pavements 400,000 vs over 500,000 sq m; underpasses 22 vs over 40; cameras over 2,000 vs about 1,240), usually because one figure counts only completed work and the other includes work in progress or district-level works. The writer should prefer the lower, more specific figure.
- The Gorna Banya bath is described on p. 84 as handed to the municipality for management in December 2025, while p. 128 still says the state must transfer ownership before reconstruction can start; the text appears not to have been reconciled.
- Much of the transport and infrastructure section describes financing and future procurement (2027–2029), i.e. outside or at the very end of the current term.
- The comparison "12.5% more in three years than the previous administration in four" is nominal; with 2022–2024 construction inflation the real difference is unclear.

### Gaps
- None of the report's financial claims could be reconciled with budget execution reports, because the report gives no budget totals.
- Independent confirmation is missing for: the EUR 220m saving, the 20-fold drop in untreated landfilling, the inspectorate statistics, the kindergarten shortfall numbers, the tree counts and the LEZ traffic counts.
