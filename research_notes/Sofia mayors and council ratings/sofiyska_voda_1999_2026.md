# Sofiyska voda water and sewerage concession, 1999–2026: condition and performance

Research date: 15 November 2026. Scope: the concession contract between Sofia Municipality and "Софийска вода" АД, year by year from signing to November 2026, for a neutral rating page on Sofia's mayors and councils.

**Evidence labels used below**
- **[DOC-REG]**: regulator document (ДКЕВР/КЕВР decision, opinion, annual report or inspection report).
- **[DOC-FS]**: the company's audited financial statements or the annual activity report filed with them.
- **[DOC-AUDIT]**: Audit Office (Сметна палата) report.
- **[DOC-COUNCIL]**: Sofia Municipal Council decision or its annex.
- **[DOC-COURT]**: court decision, known only as cited in a КЕВР decision.
- **[SELF]**: company or municipality self-reported, without regulator verification.
- **[PRESS]**: press-reported.
- **[ALLEGATION]**: a claim by a party, not a finding.
- **[DERIVED]**: my own arithmetic on documented figures (VAT, sums, differences). It is labelled every time. No missing year is estimated.

**Sources and how they were read**
- Most primary documents were downloaded to `/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-deyan-dobrinov-dev-personal-mvr/8e13c5de-2e99-44cd-a1cf-25ade74020ef/scratchpad/sv_dl/`.
- Most of the company's financial statements on sofiyskavoda.bg are scanned images. I read them by OCR (tesseract, Bulgarian). Figures taken from OCR are marked "(OCR)".
- Exact thousands from OCR were cross-checked against the next year's comparatives wherever possible.

## 0. Corrections and confirmations to what was already known

- **Confirmed:** signed 23.12.1999, in force 06.10.2000, no concession fee [DOC-AUDIT 2005].
- **Confirmed:** EBRD loan of EUR 31 m, signed 15.12.2000 [DOC-AUDIT].
  - New detail: Sofia Municipality **guaranteed the EBRD loan** with a guarantee signed on 15.12.2000.
  - The council approved that guarantee only on 19.02.2001, two months after it was signed.
  - The municipality did not book the guarantee and did not notify the Ministry of Finance.
  - Source: Audit Office report, [bulnao 1218](https://www.bulnao.government.bg/bg/documents/1218/Doklad_SofVoda_Konces.doc).
- **Correction on the "guaranteed ~17% return in the original contract".**
  - The 17% floor was written into the **Second Supplementary Agreement of 19.03.2008**, as a "Price Restriction" definition. This was done against the regulator's opinion of 27.08.2007 (Protocol 163).
  - From 2007 to 2021 ДКЕВР/КЕВР priced Sofiyska voda's services at a 17% after-tax return on equity.
  - Source: КЕВР opinion of 09.01.2023 [DOC-REG] ([pdf](https://www.dker.bg/uploads/documents/vik/stanov-kevr-4ds-prot-vik-prot10-09jan2023.pdf)).
  - Whether the 1999 original text itself contained a 17% figure was not verified. I did not read the original contract.
- **Upgraded from "allegation" to "documented": the regulator's opinion on the Fourth Supplementary Agreement (4th SA) was negative.**
  - The mayor asked for КЕВР's opinion on 09.12.2022, after the council's Decision 898 of 24.11.2022.
  - КЕВР adopted its opinion on **09.01.2023 (Protocol 10, item 1)**. It found:
    - the documents gave no complex analysis showing that the economic balance of the concession was disrupted, and no independent financial analysis by the grantor;
    - the proposed contractual 13% return on equity "по същество въвежда гарантирана договорна норма" (in substance introduces a guaranteed contractual rate), whereas under the Third SA the concessionaire had no guaranteed return after 01.01.2022;
    - the price and return clauses "не са в съответствие с ЗРВКУ и на НРЦВКУ" (do not comply with the water-services regulation act and its price ordinance).
  - The agreement was nevertheless signed on 28.08.2023.
  - So the opposition councillors' claim that it was signed despite a negative regulator opinion matches the regulator's own published text [DOC-REG].
  - Their further claim that the *council* voted without a КЕВР opinion is also consistent with the dates: the council voted on 24.11.2022 and the opinion is dated 09.01.2023. Decision 898 itself made signature conditional on the opinion being *given*, not on it being positive.
- **Supplementary agreements and their dates** [DOC-REG; DOC-COUNCIL]:

  | Agreement | Date | Mayor |

  | 1st SA | 15.12.2000 | Sofiyanski |
  | 2nd SA | 19.03.2008 | Borisov |

- **New: the courts sided with the company against КЕВР in 2022–2023** [DOC-COURT, cited in КЕВР Decision БП-Ц-4/01.10.2024].

  | First instance (Административен съд София-град) | Upheld by | What КЕВР did as a result |

  | Decision 7250 of 30.11.2022, case 6373/2021 | ВАС 8095 of 26.07.2023, case 932/2023 | КЕВР Decision **НВ-2 of 10.07.2024** set Sofiyska voda's return on equity at **13%**, "в съответствие с договореното между страните по Договора за концесия, изменен с Четвърто допълнително споразумение" (in line with the 4th SA) |

## 3. Investment: actual vs. obligation; regulator assessments and penalties

### 3.1 2000–2008 (Sofiyanski; Borisov from November 2005)
- **Contract obligation.** About 150 million in investment over 25 years. The Audit Office president said so in a 2005 interview; the currency was not stated [DOC-AUDIT, interview].
- **Upfront payment.** The concessionaire had to pay USD 1 m within 10 days of entry into force, as a deposit for losses and claims. The municipality put it in the wrong kind of account [DOC-AUDIT].
- **Audit Office, period to 31.12.2003** [DOC-AUDIT]:
  - no investment programme agreed between the municipality and the company for the whole term or for the first three years (2001–2003);
  - no report on executing it;
  - no new public assets booked to 31.12.2003;
  - the concessionaire included the full water-abstraction fee in prices, contrary to art. 193(2) of the Water Act;
  - no concession fee, contrary to the council's own 1997 ordinance;
  - the original documents were held by the monitoring firm Омонит ООД.
  - The council decision of 30.11.1998 that opened the procedure omitted the price of the concession, a mandatory element.
  - Competition result: International Water Ltd ranked first, Suez Lyonnaise des Eaux second, Vivendi/Marubeni third. The council confirmed the winner on 24.11.1999.
- **Audit Office follow-up, 2006–07** [DOC-AUDIT, bulnao 4133]. All seven recommendations were found implemented by 30.10.2005:
  - mayor's order of 21.10.2005 for annual reports on concession control;
  - a concession archive created;
  - public assets of BGN 14.2 m booked;
  - the EUR 31 m guarantee booked off-balance-sheet;
  - a water and sewerage strategy to 2020 drafted.
- **Regulator conditions in 2007** [DOC-REG, Ц-053]. The higher 2007 price (part III) was conditional on:
  - an EBRD loan of BGN 50 m with its first tranche disbursed;
  - at least BGN 15 m of investment in 2007.
  - Ц-018/26.05.2008 found all conditions met except the tranche.
- **Company figures:** investment of BGN 25.4 m in 2008 and 52.4 m in 2009 [SELF, AR 2009].

## (b) Timeline for the page (14 items)

1. **23.12.1999 (Sofiyanski)** — 25-year concession signed with International Water; no concession fee. In force 06.10.2000.
2. **15.12.2000 (Sofiyanski)** — EBRD EUR 31 m loan, guaranteed by the municipality. The council approved the guarantee only in February 2001.
3. **October 2005 (Sofiyanski → Borisov)** — Audit Office: no report to the council for four years, no agreed investment programme, documents held by a private firm. Corrective measures followed by October 2005.
4. **19.03.2008 (Borisov)** — 2nd Supplementary Agreement writes a 17% return-on-equity floor into the contract, against the regulator's 2007 opinion.
5. **2009–2013 (Borisov → Fandakova)** — First regulated business plan: BGN 240 m planned, 229 m recognised by the regulator.
6. **November 2010 (Fandakova)** — Veolia takes over the 77.1% private stake; the municipality keeps 22.9%.
7. **2014 (Fandakova)** — Regulator rejects the company's extension plan and fines it BGN 20,000.
8. **23.03.2018 (Fandakova)** — 3rd Supplementary Agreement: the 17% return to lapse from 2022; BGN 165 m minimum investment 2022–2025.
9. **2021–2024 (Fandakova → Terziev)** — The company submits no business plan for 2022–2026 while disputing the regulator's 11.12% return; prices frozen at the 2021 level.
10. **24.11.2022 (Fandakova; council 2019–2023)** — Council Decision 898 approves the 4th SA: term extended by 8 years 4 months, 13% return, BGN 634 m investment programme.
11. **09.01.2023 (Fandakova)** — The regulator finds the 4th SA's return clauses non-compliant with the law and the economic-imbalance claim unproven.
12. **28.08.2023 (Fandakova)** — 4th SA signed by a deputy mayor; the concession now runs to about February 2034.
13. **10.07.2024 – 01.10.2024 (Terziev)** — Following court rulings for the company, the regulator sets 13% return on equity and approves the 2022–2026 plan; household price +20%.
14. **2021–2025 (Fandakova → Terziev)** — After no dividends for 2015–2019 profits, BGN 198 m in dividends declared for 2020–2024 profits. Losses fell from 60.8% (2009) to 37.4% (2024).
